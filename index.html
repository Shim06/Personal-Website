About me

From a young age, I was glued to handhelds and consoles, and that constant itch to see how games actually worked and fascination with electronics pushed me straight into tech. In high school I learned HTML and CSS, later Java and Python, and then discovered the satisfaction of working closer to metal with C and C++. Delving deeper into hardware.

I’m strong in embedded systems and low-level software: C and C++ with an eye for performance and optimizations. I love with hardware and systems with constrains— and I know how to turn those constraints into a real product.

I’m always learning, diving deeper into low-level concepts, embedded systems, and electronics that help me understand and control hardware precisely. Deconstructing abstractions, I want to know why it works, where it breaks, and how to make it faster, smaller, and reliable.

What you get from me is constant adaptibility, fast learning, and discipline. I’ve built multiple low-level systems from scratch — starting with nothing but constraints and a goal— and turning them into working, reliable software. I move quickly: validate an approach, measure the bottlenecks and constraints, then make deliberate tradeoffs until the result is refined and reliable.
